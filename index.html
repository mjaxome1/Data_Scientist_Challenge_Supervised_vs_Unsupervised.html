The algorithm does not decide the problem.
You decide what question the data needs to answer.

🏷️ Supervised Learning

Data + known outcomes → learn a mapping → predict

Typical questions: “Will this customer churn?” “Is this transaction fraudulent?” “What will the price be?”

🔎 Unsupervised Learning

Data without predefined outcomes → discover structure

Typical questions: “What customer segments exist?” “Which observations are similar?”

Before choosing an algorithm, ask:

1. What exactly are we trying to accomplish?
2. Are we predicting a known outcome or discovering patterns?
3. Do we have reliable labels?
4. What information is missing?
5. How will we know whether the result is useful?
